Privacy Policy — SmartlyDigit

Effective date: 2 October 2026  ·  Contact: irobotsvc@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the SmartlyDigit Facebook application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is used solely to publish our own content (product images, descriptions and links to our Etsy shop) to the official SmartlyDigit Facebook Page, which we own and operate.

1. Who we are

SmartlyDigit sells digital downloads on Etsy (etsy.com/shop/SmartlyDigit). The App is a private automation tool that posts our own marketing content to our own Facebook Page. It is not a consumer product and does not offer sign-in, accounts, or services to the public.

2. Information we collect

The App does not collect, request, store, sell, or share any personal information from Facebook users, Page visitors, or customers.

  • We do not ask anyone to log in through the App.
  • We do not collect names, emails, contact details, payment information, location, or browsing behaviour of people who see or interact with our posts.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies through the App.

The only data the App handles is our own marketing content — the images, captions and Etsy links that we choose to publish to our own Page.

3. How the App accesses Facebook

The App uses the Facebook Graph API with a Page access token that we authorised, limited to these permissions on our own Page only:

  • pages_show_list — to identify our Page
  • pages_read_engagement — to read basic Page information
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish our content to our Page

These permissions are used exclusively to post our own content. They are not used to read, collect, or process the personal data of any other person.

4. Information we share

We do not share any personal data with third parties, because the App does not collect any. Content we publish (our product posts) is, by nature, public on our Facebook Page.

5. Data retention

The App does not maintain a database of personal information about users. The only records kept are our own scheduled and published marketing posts, stored in our own automation system, which contain no third-party personal data.

6. Data deletion

Because the App collects no personal data from users, there is no personal data to delete. If you believe we hold any information about you, or you want any content concerning you removed, contact us at irobotsvc@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

To revoke the App's access to our Facebook Page at any time, the Page owner can remove the App in Facebook Business Settings → Apps, or revoke the token in Business Settings → System Users.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL, with the effective date updated at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: irobotsvc@gmail.com
